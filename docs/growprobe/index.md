# GrowProbe User Guide

[**📄 Download the printable quickstart PDF**](/files/PEBL_GrowProbe_Quickstart_Guide_V1.pdf)

The GrowProbe is a moored water-quality sensor for farm sites. It logs temperature, turbidity, light and current (via tilt angle) to a schedule, and is set up and configured through the same PEBL App used by the SubCam.

## What you can do with a GrowProbe

* **Continuous water-quality logging.** Temperature, turbidity and light across their full operating range.
* **Current speed and direction from tilt.** Reports tilt angle (0 to 85°, roughly 0 to 2.5 knots) and heading (0 to 360°).
* **Flexible logging rate.** From 10 readings a second to one a day, with burst and averaging modes.
* **Built for the sea.** POM-C enclosure with titanium hardware, rated for continuous submersion to 50 m.
* **Long deployments.** Up to 320 days on a single charge at a 30-minute sampling rate.

## Set up in four steps

1. **Power up.** Unscrew the float to reach the power button, then press and hold it for 5 seconds.
2. **[Connect to the PEBL App](./connect-and-configure.md)** over the GrowProbe's own WiFi network.
3. **Configure your settings.** Calibrate the compass and turbidity sensor, then set your deployment details and schedule.
4. **Deploy.** Screw the float back on and deploy the probe.
