# Operating and retrieving data

## App overview

The PEBL App home screen shows device status and gives you the main controls.

![PEBL App home screen with numbered controls.](/img/subcam-app-overview.png)

1. Battery charge status
2. Signal strength
3. Turn off
4. Custom device name
5. Device serial number
6. Device status
7. Stop logging
8. Configure and launch
9. Manage data files
10. Manage media files

## Stop logging

1. In the PEBL App, press **Stop** to end the logging session.
2. Turn the device off, either from the app or by pressing the power button on the device.

## Transferring files to a computer

1. Stop the device logging, and make sure the device is **off**.
2. Remove the SD card from the SubCam and insert it into your computer.

:::note
Media and data files can also be browsed in the PEBL App, from the **Media** and **Data** buttons on the home screen.
:::
