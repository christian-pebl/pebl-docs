# Connect and configure

:::tip
This is the quick version for the SubCam. For the full app reference (every setting, the schedule builder, and data and media management), see the [PEBL App guide](./pebl-app/index.md).
:::

## Connect to the PEBL App

1. Press and hold the **power button for 5 seconds**, then wait about 20 seconds.
2. On your phone or laptop, join the SubCam WiFi network. It is named after the device serial number, for example `Subcam-D0001`.
3. Open [peblapp.co.uk](https://peblapp.co.uk) (or `10.0.0.1:8000`) in any browser.

![Connecting to the SubCam: join its WiFi network, then open the PEBL App in a browser.](/img/subcam-connect.png)

:::info
No internet or mobile signal is needed. The PEBL App runs entirely over the SubCam's own WiFi network, so it works anywhere, including offshore.
:::

## Configure and start logging

1. With the PEBL App open, go to **Configure**.
2. Edit the camera and device settings.
3. Add a custom schedule with **Add Schedule**. Capture once every 30 minutes, hourly, only between set hours, or whatever pattern suits your deployment.
4. Press **Save and start logging**.

The status LED turns green, and the device shuts down and enters logging mode within 30 seconds.

:::tip
Plan your schedule against the 15-hour recording budget. Spreading short captures across the day extends a deployment over weeks or months. Continuous capture uses the budget in a single session.
:::
