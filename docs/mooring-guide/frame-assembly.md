The video shows the **Med exposure** frame (500 × 500 × 350 mm, 42.4 mm tube). The **Low exposure** frame is the same build in 33.7 mm tube. The **High exposure** frame adds two guard rails, so check its parts list on the [High Exposure](./frame-configurations/high-exposure.md) page before you start.

## What you need

| Part | Qty |
| --- | --- |
| Long tube | 7 (2 seabed rails, 2 cross rails, 2 H sides, 1 H crossbar) |
| Short tube | 4 (legs) |
| 90° elbow (Interclamp 125) | 8 |
| Short tee (Interclamp 101) | 6 |
| Allen key | 1 |

Work on flat ground.

## Steps

1. **Lay out all the parts.** Check everything is there before you start.
2. **Build the two seabed rails.** Put an elbow loosely on each end of two long tubes, with the sockets pointing up.
3. **Stand the four legs in the elbows.** Push each leg fully down. If it stops short, slide the rail back a little.
4. **Slide on the two cross rails.** Put a tee on each end of two long tubes. The tees slide down the legs to halfway, joining the two seabed rails.
5. **Lightly tighten everything so far.** Just snug for now. You square it up at the end.
6. **Build the top H.** Slide a tee to the middle of two long tubes (the sides of the H) and join the tees with a third long tube (the crossbar). Put an elbow on each end of the sides, pointing down. Lightly tighten.
7. **Lower the H onto the legs.** The sides of the H sit directly over the seabed rails. Push each leg fully into its elbow. If one stops short, slide that side of the H back a little.
8. **Check it is flat and straight.** On flat ground, all four corners touch down and nothing is twisted.
9. **Tighten every screw with the Allen key.** Start at the bottom and work up, going clockwise. Firm, not forced, because too much force rounds off the Allen key.

:::warning
Check every screw is firm before you deploy.
:::

Next, mount the SubCam on the crossbar and set its tilt. See [Camera Configuration](./camera-configuration.md).
