# High Exposure Mooring Example

> **Example:** 20 m depth at high tide   |   &lt;10 m wave height   |   &lt;4 m/s current speed

<div style={{maxWidth: '560px', margin: '0 auto 1.5rem'}}>

![High exposure mooring in 20 m of water at high tide. A 24 m floating rope arches from the lander to a 50 kg clump chain. An S-shaped rope, 24 m floating then 24 m sinking, runs from the chain up to two surface buoys 2 m apart.](/img/rigging-high.png)

</div>

Every rope is as long as the depth at high tide plus 20%, so 24 m here, apart from the 2 m between the buoys. Striped rope floats and solid rope sinks. The ropes and the depth are drawn to scale. The lander, chain and buoys are not.

The [deployment video](../deployment.md) shows how to put this rig in the water. Its example is 10 m deep, so its ropes are 12 m.
