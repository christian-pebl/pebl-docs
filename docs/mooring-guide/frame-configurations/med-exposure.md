# Med Exposure Mooring Example

> **Example:** 10 m depth at high tide   |   &lt;5 m wave height   |   &lt;2 m/s current speed

<div style={{maxWidth: '560px', margin: '0 auto 1.5rem'}}>

![Med exposure mooring in 10 m of water at high tide. A 12 m floating rope arches from the lander to a 25 kg clump chain. An S-shaped rope, 12 m floating then 12 m sinking, runs from the chain up to two surface buoys 2 m apart.](/img/rigging-med.png)

</div>

Every rope is as long as the depth at high tide plus 20%, so 12 m here. Striped rope floats and solid rope sinks. The ropes and the depth are drawn to scale. The lander, chain and buoys are not.

This is the rig in the [deployment video](../deployment.md), which shows how to put it in the water.
