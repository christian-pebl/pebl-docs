The video works through one example, a site that is **10 m deep at high tide**. The rope lengths follow from the depth, so work them out for your own site.

## The rig, in the order it goes in

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Lander | The frame with its SubCam fitted |
| Bridle | Two ropes tied to opposite sides of the frame, joined at a small 2 litre buoy |
| Rope | Floating rope, as long as the depth at high tide plus 20%. That is 12 m in the example |
| Clump chain | 25 kg, or 50 kg on an exposed site |
| S-shaped rope | Floating rope joined to sinking rope, each as long as the first rope. That is 12 m and 12 m in the example |
| Buoys | Two small surface buoys, 2 m apart |

## Steps

1. **Tie a bridle to the frame.** Two ropes, on opposite sides. Join them at a small 2 litre buoy.
2. **Add rope and a clump chain.** Tie a floating rope to the bridle. Its length is the depth at high tide, plus 20%. Tie the clump chain to its other end. Use a 25 kg chain, or 50 kg on an exposed site.
3. **Then an S-shaped rope and two buoys.** Join a floating rope to a sinking rope, each as long as the first rope. Tie the floating end to the chain. Tie two small buoys to the sinking end, 2 m apart.
4. **Lay it out in drop order.** Lander first, buoys last. Flake the ropes so they run free.
5. **At the site, check the drift.** Stop and see which way the boat drifts. You lay the rig along that line.
6. **Lower the lander slowly.** Run the rope over a block for a little friction and ease it out by hand. A light lander can go over the side by hand.
7. **Feel it land, then give slack.** Let the rope go slack the moment it lands.
8. **Drop the clump chain.** Have it ready and drop it straight away. Close to the lander, never on top. In the example that is about 5 m away.
9. **Let out the S rope, then the buoys.** Throw the buoys over, then mark the position.

:::warning
A tight rope pulls the lander over. Give slack the moment you feel it land.
:::

Once it is down, the floating rope arches up off the seabed between the lander and the chain. The S-shaped rope stops the buoys tugging the chain.
