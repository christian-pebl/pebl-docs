# Camera Configuration

The SubCam is mounted on the central crossbar of a galvanised steel pipe **'Microlander'** frame. Camera tilt, field of view, seabed slant range and reach are configured before deployment. The example below shows a typical near-shore configuration at **-15° tilt** with **2 m water visibility**.

:::info
SubCam cameras give a **Field of View of 100° horizontally and 60° vertically**.
:::

The image below shows the central focus and projected field of view onto the seabed relative to the visibility range.
