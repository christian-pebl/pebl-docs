# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Plant Ecology Beyond Land (PEBL) CIC ("PEBL", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data. It covers our website, these docs, the fleet portal, the PEBL data cloud, and the way we handle email, calls and orders. FishSpotter has its own notice, explained below. We are the data controller.

**Effective date:** 4 October 2026

**Visiting the site.** Our host, Netlify, processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages to you and to protect the site from attack. We rely on legitimate interests (running a secure website). Netlify processes this in the United States.

**Forms.** When you use a form on our website, such as the order form, an enquiry form or the newsletter sign-up, we collect what you enter. That can include your name, email address, phone number, organisation, delivery address, purchase order number and message. Netlify stores each submission for us and may record technical details with it, such as your IP address and browser. Each submission is also emailed to us (see Email, phone calls and orders below).

* **Why:** to handle your order, answer your enquiry, or send the newsletter you asked for.
* **Lawful basis:** contract, for orders, including the steps you ask us to take before we confirm one; legitimate interests, for enquiries; consent, for the newsletter.
* **Processed by:** Netlify, in the United States.

**Website statistics.** Once our new website is live, we measure how it is used so that we can improve it.

* Cloudflare Web Analytics shows us which pages people view, which websites refer them to us, their country and type of device, and how fast pages load. It does not use cookies and does not follow you across other websites.
* We also count page requests ourselves on Netlify, by page address and campaign tag, and count visits from automated crawlers. These counts hold no IP addresses or browser details.
* You can switch statistics off for your browser at [www.pebl-cic.co.uk/privacy-choices](https://www.pebl-cic.co.uk/privacy-choices). If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request to switch them off.
* Cloudflare and Netlify process this data only for us. The law allows this kind of measurement without asking first, as long as we explain it and give you a simple way to switch it off.
* **Lawful basis:** legitimate interests (understanding and improving our website).
* **Processed by:** Cloudflare and Netlify, in the United States.

**Booking a call.** If you book a call through our Calendly link, Calendly collects your name, email address, the time you choose and anything you add to the booking, and passes the booking to our calendar.

* **Lawful basis:** legitimate interests (arranging the call you asked for).
* **Processed by:** Calendly, in the United States.

**Videos.** Some pages show YouTube videos. The preview picture is loaded from YouTube, so YouTube can see your IP address and browser details when the page opens. The video itself loads only if you press play, and YouTube may then store information on your device. YouTube is part of Google, and Google's own privacy policy covers its use of this data.

### These docs, docs.pebl-cic.co.uk

This site has no forms, sets no cookies and runs no analytics tools. Our host, Netlify, processes your IP address and browser details in the United States to deliver pages and protect the site, and we rely on legitimate interests (running a secure website). If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers the choice on your device. It is not sent to us.

### The fleet portal, peblapp.co.uk

The fleet portal is where customers see and manage their PEBL kit and its data.

* **Data:** your name, email address, username and organisation, and your password, which is stored in a form we cannot read. Records of which kit you hold, and data from that kit, including device settings, status and logs, sensor readings, deployment records (where and when kit was deployed) and the footage and images it captures. Footage can sometimes show people, boats or places that could identify someone.
* **Why:** to give you access to your kit and its data, to check that kit is working, to support you, and to improve our kit and service.
* **Lawful basis:** contract, for providing the portal to customers; legitimate interests, for device health checks, support, security and product improvement.
* **AI log review:** where we have switched it on, the portal can send short extracts of device logs, with any automatic fault findings, to Anthropic's AI service, which suggests what may have gone wrong. We rely on legitimate interests (finding and fixing faults).
* **Processed by:** Render, which hosts the portal and its database in Oregon, United States; Cloudflare R2, which stores footage, images, archived logs and backups (storage location **[TO CONFIRM: R2 bucket location]**); and Anthropic, in the United States, for the AI log review.

### The PEBL data cloud, pebl-data.cloud

The data cloud is our partner sign-in for storing, viewing and sharing monitoring data.

* **Data:** your name, email address and role; the projects, data files, notes and map locations you add or that are shared with you; and records of sign-ins and access.
* **Why:** to let you store, view and analyse data, and to share it with the people you choose.
* **Lawful basis:** contract, where you use it under an agreement with us; legitimate interests otherwise, for example for partners on joint projects.
* **Processed by:** Vercel, which hosts the app, with its server functions in the United States (near Washington DC); and Supabase, which provides the database, sign-in and file storage, held in Stockholm, Sweden.

### Email, phone calls and orders

* **Data:** your name and contact details, what you tell us, and records of quotes, orders, hires, invoices and payments.
* **Why:** to reply to you, to supply and support your kit, and to keep the business and tax records the law requires.
* **Lawful basis:** contract, for orders and hires; legitimate interests, for enquiries and support; legal obligation, for tax and accounting records.
* **Processed by:** Google, through Google Workspace, which provides our email, calendar and file storage. Google may process this data in the United States and other countries. ### The PEBL App on your kit

The PEBL App runs on the kit itself over a local WiFi network, with no internet connection, and sends nothing to us. If your kit is set up to upload to the fleet portal, what it uploads is handled as described above.

## International transfers

Some of our providers process data outside the UK. We only transfer it with a safeguard that UK law recognises.

* **The European Economic Area.** UK adequacy regulations recognise the EEA as protecting personal data adequately.
* **US companies certified under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.** UK adequacy regulations, often called the UK-US data bridge, cover transfers to certified companies.
* **Other providers.** We use the EU standard contractual clauses with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, which commit the provider to protecting your data in line with UK law.

If a provider's certification lapses, we will put another lawful safeguard in place and update this policy. Email us if you would like a copy of the safeguard for any provider.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

* get a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
* have it corrected if it is wrong;
* have it deleted;
* restrict how we use it;
* object to how we use it, including for direct marketing and website statistics;
* receive the data you gave us in a format you can reuse, or have it sent to another organisation;
* withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent; and
* not be subject to decisions made solely by automated means that significantly affect you. We do not make decisions like that.

Some of these rights depend on the lawful basis we rely on, and some have exceptions. To use any of them, email [hello@pebl-cic.co.uk](mailto:hello@pebl-cic.co.uk). We reply within one month, or tell you within that month if a complex request needs longer. In most cases there is no charge.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please tell us. The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 gives you the right to complain to us directly.

1. Send your complaint by email to [hello@pebl-cic.co.uk](mailto:hello@pebl-cic.co.uk), by post to our registered office, or by phone on 07905 672766.
2. We acknowledge your complaint within 30 days of receiving it.
3. We look into it, keep you informed, and tell you the outcome without undue delay.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the ICO at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or on 0303 123 1113.
