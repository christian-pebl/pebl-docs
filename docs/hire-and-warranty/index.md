# Hire & Warranty

The commercial terms that apply to PEBL kit, whether you are renting it or you have bought it.

There are two documents here, and which one applies depends on how you got the kit:

* **[Hire Terms and Conditions](./hire-terms.md)** apply when you **rent** kit from PEBL for a fixed period and return it.
* **[Warranty](./warranty.md)** applies when you have **bought** kit and own it.

:::info[How hire works]
The Hire Terms are the standard terms that apply to every hire. The specific kit, hire period and charges for your hire are set out in a separate **Hire Confirmation** that PEBL issues to you, which refers to these standard terms. Placing a hire order means you accept the Hire Terms.
:::

These pages use the PEBL SubCam as the worked example. The same terms apply to other PEBL kit unless your Hire Confirmation or order says otherwise.
